Hilt operates a curated marketplace enabling travelers to discover and reserve homestays, cottages, and heritage mountain dwellings directly from verified local hosts across Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Ladakh.

3. Bookings and Payouts

  • Guest Reservations: When a guest reserves a stay, payments are held securely in an RBI-compliant escrow account until 24 hours post-check-in.

  • Statutory Taxes (Section 194-O TDS): In accordance with Indian tax laws, Hilt deducts applicable TDS on gross booking value (0.1% for PAN-verified hosts exceeding ₹5,00,000 gross annual turnover; 5% under Section 206AA for accounts without a verified PAN).

4. 24-Hour Payout Escrow Hold

Every confirmed booking through Hilt is secured by an automated 24-hour payment escrow hold:

  • Guest payments are held securely in an RBI-compliant escrow account and released to the host 24 hours after check-in.
  • Travelers are protected by having 24 hours post check-in to verify that the accommodation exists and is accessible before funds are released.
  • Hosts are protected by guaranteed upfront payment held in escrow, eliminating cash disputes or unfulfilled reservations.
  • Hilt operates solely as a digital technology marketplace and does not act as an insurer or underwrite property damage guarantees. Direct damages or house rule violations are subject to mutual resolution between host and guest.

5. Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • Cancellation refunds follow the specific policy (Flexible, Moderate, or Strict) selected by the host and clearly displayed prior to booking confirmation.
  • Eligible refunds are processed directly to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
