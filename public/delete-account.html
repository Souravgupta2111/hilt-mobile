Hilt Account & Data Deletion Request

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), and Google Play Data Safety policies, you have the absolute right to request permanent deletion of your Hilt account and all associated personal data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

  1. Open the Hilt app on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Settings section.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm the prompt.

Your account, auth credentials, saved properties, and profile are immediately and permanently erased.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit your request below or email privacy@hilt.travel with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Thank you. Your request has been received. Our data compliance officer will verify and purge all records associated with this account within 48 hours.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Your personal authentication record (Supabase Auth).
  • Your user profile, name, phone number, and avatar.
  • Your saved wishlists and trip drafts.
  • Host listings (if any) are delisted.
  • Financial invoices are retained in anonymized format solely for mandatory Indian GST and Income Tax statutory retention periods.
