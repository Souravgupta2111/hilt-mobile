Hilt Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · Compliant with DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR

Welcome to Hilt ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Hilt Technologies Private Limited. We connect travelers directly with authentic local homestay hosts across the Himalayan valleys (Kullu, Spiti, Kinnaur, Ladakh, Tirthan, and Parvati). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and your personal data.

Your Data Rights at a Glance: You can view, export, modify, or permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the Hilt app at any time, or via our web portal at hilt.travel/delete-account.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide authentic travel experiences, secure payment processing, and safety guarantees:

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, phone number, and encrypted authentication tokens provided during sign up or social sign-in (Google / Apple).
  • Booking & Trip Information: Stays booked, check-in and check-out dates, guest counts, and special accommodation requests.
  • Host Verification & Payout Data: For hosts listing stays, we collect Government ID verification (Aadhaar / Passport under DPDP-compliant encryption), PAN number for Indian Income Tax TDS (Section 194-O) compliance, and bank account / UPI details for automated settlement.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Push notification tokens (Expo Push), IP address for fraud detection, and anonymous crash telemetry.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your personal data solely for the following lawful purposes:

  • Facilitating reservations between guests and Himalayan hosts.
  • Processing payments and GST invoices via RBI-licensed payment gateways (Razorpay).
  • Distributing host earnings and deducting statutory TDS under Section 194-O of the Indian Income Tax Act.
  • Administering the automated 24-hour payment escrow hold between guests and hosts.
  • Sending transactional SMS and push alerts regarding reservation confirmations and check-in instructions.

3. Payment Gateway & Third-Party Processors

Hilt does not store your credit card, debit card, or net banking credentials on our servers. All financial transactions are securely processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-authorized Payment Aggregator adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

4. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain your personal information only as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable Indian statutory regulations (such as GST and Income Tax retention requirements for financial invoices). When you request account deletion:

  • Your personal profile, contact information, photos, and saved properties are immediately and permanently erased from our active databases.
  • Historical financial invoices are anonymized and retained solely for mandatory tax compliance audits.

5. How to Delete Your Account (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • In-App: Open the Hilt app → go to Profile → scroll to Account Settings → tap Delete Account and confirm. Your account and personal profile will be instantly deleted.
  • Web: Visit hilt.travel/delete-account or email our Data Protection Officer at privacy@hilt.travel with your registered email or mobile number.
